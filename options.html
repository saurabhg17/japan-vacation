Ochanomizu, Jimbocho and Akihabara (walkable, half day)

Ochanomizu Guitar Street

Meidai-dori from the JR Ochanomizu-bashi exit: dozens of new and used instrument shops. Ishibashi Music: Mon–Sat 11:00–20:00, Sun 10:00–19:00. Ask for tax-free with your passport.

Sports shops (Kanda-Ogawamachi / Jimbocho)

Street of sports and ski shops, including Victoria, Xebio and the Mizuno flagship, at the bottom of Guitar Street. Check: if "Bingo" meant the Bingo used-clothing store, that one is in Shibuya (Udagawacho, 5 min from Hachiko exit).

Akihabara

Electronics, parts shops, anime and arcades. 10–15 min walk east from Ochanomizu, or one stop on JR.

Asakusa and Ginza

Kimono experience, AsakusaBook

Book a morning slot, then wear it to Senso-ji. Shops dress you, you walk around for a few hours, then return the kimono.

G.Itoya Ginza

Twelve-floor stationery shop. ~10 min from Akihabara on the Hibiya line, or ~20 min from Asakusa on the Ginza line.

Basics

  • Getting to the hotel: Shinagawa → JR Yamanote to Shinjuku (~20 min). Use the East Exit, go through the red Kabukicho Ichibangai arch and walk to the Godzilla-head tower. 5–10 min on foot. No taxi needed.
  • Sunset: about 16:28 from 28 Nov to 1 Dec. Dark by about 17:00, so do gardens and outdoor views before then.
  • Closed days: Shinjuku Gyoen, Kakimori and Pigment close on Monday, and so do the Ueno museums. Kappabashi closes on Sunday.
  • Fuji: the best day is Mon 30 Nov. It is a weekday and the forecast is clear (−2 to 13°C). See Mt Fuji.

Walk from the hotel (Shinjuku)

All within about 15 min on foot.

Kabukicho neon and the Godzilla headToday

Best after dark. The hotel's 8F Godzilla terrace is reported closed for now, so see the head from the street.

Omoide YokochoToday

Lantern-lit alleys by the station, good for a walk-through and photos. The food is mostly yakitori, so not for the adults to eat.

Golden GaiToday

Six lanes of tiny bars, about 6 min from the hotel. Worth a walk for the atmosphere. Many bars charge a seat fee.

TMG Observatory (South)TodayFree

202 m view. South deck open 09:30–22:00, last entry 30 min before closing. You may see Fuji's outline at sunset on a clear day. A free light show is projected on the building's front in the evening. ~15 min walk west.

Shinjuku Gyoen

Big garden with autumn maples. 09:00–16:30, last entry 16:00. Closed Mondays. ¥500 adults, ¥250 high-school students, free for 15 and under. Best Sunday morning; today only if you go straight after check-in.

Tokyo's oldest temple and Nakamise street. Go before 09:00 to avoid crowds.

Kakimori

Park, zoo and national museums. Museums closed Mondays.

Akihabara

Mt Fuji and Lake Kawaguchiko (full day)

Day trip from Shinjuku. Best day: Mon 30 Nov. Fuji is usually clearest early in the day, so leave early.

North-shore park with Fuji across the lake. Take the sightseeing bus from Kawaguchiko station.

Short cable car to a viewpoint over the lake and Fuji. Near Kawaguchiko station.

Lake Kawaguchi cruise

About 20 min on the water. Easy with kids.

Oshino Hakkai

Village with clear spring ponds. Worth it even if Fuji is in cloud. Separate bus from Kawaguchiko.

Momiji Kairo (Maple Corridor)

The festival ended 29 Nov and the leaves are likely past their best. Stop only if you pass by.

Fuji-Q Highland

Big theme park with roller coasters. It replaces the sightseeing loop rather than adding to it.

Fuji 5th Station

Don't plan around it. The road may close for snow and ice from late November.

Stay overnight at Kawaguchiko? Not for this trip. The forecast for Monday is clear, so a day trip leaving early will very likely see Fuji. An overnight stay would mean cancelling or wasting a Gracery night, moving bags twice, and booking a Fuji-view ryokan at short notice. Ryokan dinners would also need a vegetarian request in advance. It is only worth it for a sunrise view, an onsen evening, or a second chance if the first day clouds over.

Tours

Sky Hop Bus (hop-on hop-off)Skip

Three routes: Red (Asakusa/Skytree), Blue (Tokyo Tower/Ginza) and Green (Shinjuku/Shibuya). From ~¥3,800 per adult. The open top is cold after dark, it is slow in traffic, and the trains reach the same places faster.

Guided Fuji bus tourConsider

The one tour worth paying for, because the Fuji sights are spread out. See the table above.
