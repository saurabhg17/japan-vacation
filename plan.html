Kyoto · Day plan

Thu 26 Nov

Arashiyama, the Sagano train and Kinkaku-ji

Start 10:30 from the hotel. Back at the hotel by about 20:30. Dry and sunny, high around 13°C, so bring a warm layer for the open train and the evening.

Before you leave: book or check the Sagano train (see Trains below). Carry cash: the Tenryu-ji garden fee and the train counter do not take cards. Shigetsu shojin lunch is closed on Thursdays, so lunch is at a vegetarian cafe instead.

Timeline

  1. 10:30
    Hotel to Kyoto Station (walk ~15 min to JR platforms 31–33, west end), then JR Sagano line to Saga-Arashiyama, ~15 min. Tap Suica/ICOCA.
  2. 11:10

    Tenryu-ji garden

    Sogen pond garden, ~45 min. ¥500 cash. Leave by the north gate.

  3. 11:50

    Bamboo Grove

    The north gate opens straight onto it. 30 min. Busy at this hour; keep moving and photograph from the side paths.

  4. 12:30

    Vegetarian lunch

    Pick one from the lunch list below. All are within 10 min of the station.

  5. 13:45
    Torokko Saga station, next to JR Saga-Arashiyama. Be there 15 min early.
  6. 14:02

    Sagano Romantic Train

    Saga to Kameoka, arrive 14:25. Sit on the river side. The 13:02 also works if you are early.

  7. 14:30
    Walk ~10 min to JR Umahori, local train to Enmachi (~20 min), then taxi ~10 min to Kinkaku-ji.
  8. 15:30

    Kinkaku-ji

    The pond view of the Golden Pavilion. Crowds thin after 15:30 and the low sun lights the gold. Open 09:00–17:00, ~1 h.

  9. 16:45
    Taxi to Kyoto Station, ~30 min in evening traffic.
  10. 17:15

    Dinner at Kyoto Station

    Restaurant floors in the station building and Porta underground. Use the dietary card.

  11. 18:45

    To-ji autumn light-up

    Five-storey pagoda and maples lit up and reflected in the pond. 18:00–21:30, last entry 21:00. ¥1,000 adults, ¥500 children. ~15 min walk or a short taxi from the station; ~20 min walk back to the hotel.

Sagano tickets: buy e-tickets on the official site (sagano-kanko.co.jp). If online is sold out, the Torokko Saga counter sells same-day tickets first-come, first-served from about 08:35, cash or IC card only. After seats sell out they sell a limited number of standing tickets. If nothing is left, skip the train and take a taxi from Arashiyama straight to Kinkaku-ji (~25 min), adding Ryoan-ji next door.

Lunch in Arashiyama (vegetarian)

Musubi CafeBest for the family

Labelled vegetarian lunch plate; also serves meat for the kids. Short walk from the JR station.

Saga Tofu Ine

Tofu set meals with vegan items marked on the menu. Near the Tenryu-ji gate.

Ms Bento

Vegan, two dishes only (curry or sushi bento). By Saga-Arashiyama station. Cash only.

Check today's hours in Google Maps before walking over.

Evening options

To-ji light-upPick

Closest to the hotel and another pond-and-pagoda view. In the timeline above.

Kyoto Station Skyway and shops

Free glass walkway on the top floor, plus Isetan and Porta. Easy if the kids are tired.

Pontocho, Kiyamachi and the Shijo arcades

Lantern-lit alley by the river, and the covered Teramachi and Shinkyogoku shopping streets, open into the evening. 10 min by taxi from the station.

Kiyomizu-dera light-up

Runs to 30 Nov, until 21:30. Better kept for Friday's kimono day in Higashiyama.

Night markets: Kyoto has no evening street markets like a pasar malam. Nishiki Market is daytime and most stalls close by early evening. For evening shopping use the Shijo arcades or Kyoto Station.
