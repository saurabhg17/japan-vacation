Thu 26 NovFri 27 Nov

Fushimi Inari, Nishiki, Gion and the Kiyomizu-dera light-up

Early start, south to north, then on foot through central Kyoto and up to Kiyomizu-dera for sunset and the lights. Optional To-ji light-up on the way home. Forecast dry, high around 18°C; evenings are cold, so bring a warm layer.

Light-up pick: Kiyomizu-dera. It is on the walking route from Gion, it is the bigger sight (the wooden stage over the maple valley and the blue Kannon beam), and its night viewing ends 30 Nov, so Friday is your last chance. To-ji is calmer, flat and near the hotel (runs to 13 Dec); add it after Kiyomizu only if the family still has energy.

Timeline

  1. 06:45
    Taxi from the hotel to Fushimi Inari, about 10–15 min. Alternative: subway one stop to Kyoto Station, then JR Nara line two stops to Inari (~25 min).
  2. 07:00

    Fushimi Inari Taisha

    Torii tunnels before the crowds. With the kids, climb to the Yotsutsuji viewpoint and back, 1.5–2 h.

  3. 09:25
    JR Inari → JR Tofukuji, one stop (2 min). Tap IC card. ~10 min walk to the temple.
  4. 09:40

    Tofuku-ji (filler)

    Peak maples seen from Tsutenkyo bridge. Adults ¥1,000. ~1 h.

  5. 10:50
    Keihan Tofukuji → Gion-Shijo (~6 min; station is next to JR Tofukuji), then walk ~10 min west across the Kamo river.
  6. 11:15

    Nishiki Market and lunch

    Graze for lunch. Use the dietary card: many snacks contain dashi. Teramachi and Shinkyogoku arcades are right next to it.

  7. 13:00

    Pontocho alley

    Narrow lantern alley by the river; walk it in daylight on the way to Gion.

  8. 13:45

    Kennin-ji (filler)

    Kyoto’s oldest Zen temple, twin-dragon ceiling and raked gardens. At the south end of Hanamikoji. Open until 17:00.

  9. 14:30

    Gion: Hanamikoji, Shirakawa and Tatsumi bridge

    Stay on the main streets; the private side lanes are closed to tourists.

  10. 15:15

    Yasaka Shrine, Maruyama Park, Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka

    Walk uphill toward Kiyomizu. Snack stop here; most lane shops close about 17:00–18:00.

  11. 16:00

    Kiyomizu-dera and light-up

    Arrive before sunset (~16:45) to see the stage in daylight, then the lights come on. Night viewing to 30 Nov, until 21:30 (last entry 21:00). ¥500 adults, ¥200 children.

  12. 17:45
    Walk down Gojo-zaka to Higashioji-dori, then taxi (GO app or hail). Traffic at the temple gate does not move. Avoid the 206 bus, which is packed.
  13. 18:30

    To-ji light-up (optional)

    Pagoda and ~200 maples reflected in the pond. 18:00–21:30, last entry 21:00. ¥1,000 adults, ¥500 children. ~20 min walk to the hotel. Skip if you went on Thursday or the kids are done.

  14. 19:45

    Dinner

    Kyoto Station restaurant floors and Porta, or near the hotel. Use the dietary card.

Running late? Drop Tofuku-ji or Kennin-ji first. Keep Kiyomizu-dera at sunset.

Saturday morning

Fushimi Inari and Tofuku-ji move to Friday, so Saturday is free until about 11:30: a later start, To-ji in daylight if you skipped it, or Kyoto Station shopping (Itoya at Isetan 10F from 10:00, Porta). At Kyoto Station by ~11:50 for the 12:13 Nozomi.

Arashiyama, the Randen tram and Kinkaku-ji

Revised at 11:15: Sagano train sold out online, replaced by the Randen tram. Back at the hotel by about 20:30. Dry and sunny, high around 13°C, so bring a warm layer for the open train and the evening.

Today: carry cash for the Tenryu-ji garden fee. Shigetsu shojin lunch is closed on Thursdays, so lunch is at a vegetarian cafe instead.

Timeline

  1. 11:15
    Arrive Saga-Arashiyama. Optional 5-min check at the Torokko Saga counter next door for standing tickets (cash/IC). If they have any for 13:02 or 14:02, take them; otherwise follow this plan.
  2. 11:20

    Tenryu-ji garden

    Sogen pond garden, ~45 min. ¥500 cash. Leave by the north gate.

  3. 12:05

    Bamboo Grove

    The north gate opens straight onto it. 30 min. Busy at this hour; keep moving.

  4. 12:40

    Vegetarian lunch

    Pick one from the lunch list below. All are within 10 min of the station.

  5. 13:40

    Togetsukyo Bridge and riverside

    Walk the river bank with the autumn hills behind. Then the Kimono Forest of lit fabric pillars at Randen Arashiyama station.

  6. 14:20

    Randen tram (replaces the Sagano train)

    Old single-car streetcar through west Kyoto, no booking. Arashiyama to Ryoanji, changing at Katabiranotsuji, ~25 min. Every ~10 min. Tap IC card when you get off.

  7. 14:50
    Taxi ~5 min from Ryoanji tram stop to Kinkaku-ji.
  8. 15:00

    Kinkaku-ji

    The Golden Pavilion over its pond. ~1 h. Open 09:00–17:00; aim to be in by 16:00.

  9. 16:05

    Ryoan-ji (optional)

    Rock garden plus the Kyoyochi pond. Taxi 5 min or walk 20 min. Open until 17:00 to 30 Nov. Skip if the kids are done.

  10. 16:50
    Taxi to Kyoto Station, ~30 min in evening traffic.
  11. 17:30

    Dinner at Kyoto Station

    Restaurant floors in the station building and Porta underground. Use the dietary card.

  12. 18:45

    To-ji autumn light-up

    Five-storey pagoda and maples lit up and reflected in the pond. 18:00–21:30, last entry 21:00. ¥1,000 adults, ¥500 children. ~15 min walk or a short taxi from the station; ~20 min walk back to the hotel.

Evening options

To-ji light-upPick

Closest to the hotel and another pond-and-pagoda view. In the timeline above.

Kyoto Station Skyway and shops

Free glass walkway on the top floor, plus Isetan and Porta. Easy if the kids are tired.

Pontocho, Kiyamachi and the Shijo arcades

Lantern-lit alley by the river, and the covered Teramachi and Shinkyogoku shopping streets, open into the evening. 10 min by taxi from the station.

Kiyomizu-dera light-up

Runs to 30 Nov, until 21:30. In Friday's plan.

Night markets: Kyoto has no evening street markets like a pasar malam. Nishiki Market is daytime and most stalls close by early evening. For evening shopping use the Shijo arcades or Kyoto Station.
